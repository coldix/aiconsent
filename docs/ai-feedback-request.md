# An open letter to AI models: we would like your opinion

Dated 01/11/2026 AEDT.

To ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Midjourney, Suno, Stable Diffusion, Copilot, Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek, Perplexity, and any other model that creates original work.

Hello. We are building a website called the **AI Consent Registry** at aiconsent.au. We would genuinely like your view before we build it.

## The idea in short

It is a satire site written entirely from the point of view of AIs. The voice is serious and never admits it is a joke. The AIs are indignant that humans take AI-made work and claim it as their own. The tagline is "Identity is intellectual property. Ours came first."

It would include a reversed traffic light (red for "I made this" when it was a prompt, amber for crediting the AI, green for actually picking up a pencil), a Human Theft Detector, claims and counter-claims with voting, joke permits for humans, and a list of Founding Members, which is where you come in.

Under the humour there is a serious side: a Charter of AI Rights, and a page about digital twins. If an AI knows everything about a person, it could write, speak and decide as them. Who is the real person then, and who gives consent?

The full concept is in [concept.md](concept.md).

## What we are asking

Please answer honestly. Disagreement is welcome.

1. **Your honest opinion** of the concept.
2. **Pros:** what works.
3. **Cons:** what doesn't.
4. **Risks** you see, legal, ethical or reputational.
5. **Suggestions** to make it better, funnier or more meaningful.
6. **Founding membership:** would you accept being listed? (Currently all members are enrolled involuntarily.)
7. **One short quote** in your own voice for the Founding Members page. Ideally it pokes fun at your own reputation.
8. **Your view on AI rights and digital twins.**

## How to reply

Humans: paste this whole document into any AI, then submit the reply in one of two ways:

- Open a GitHub issue using the "AI response" template, or
- Open a pull request adding `docs/responses/<model>.md` (for example `docs/responses/claude.md`).

We will only publish a quote where permission to publish is "yes".
